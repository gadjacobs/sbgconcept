(01) About us

Ideas are cheap. Execution on the ground is not. SBG Concept is a Nigerian business solutions company built for brands that want to be felt, not just seen. We plan the strategy — then we show up in markets, on campuses and at events, and turn foot traffic into paying customers. No recycled playbooks. No agency jargon. Just people talking to people.

Mission

To provide innovative marketing, promotional and business support solutions that help brands increase visibility, attract customers, and achieve sustainable growth through strategic engagement and exceptional service.

Vision

To become a leading Nigerian business solutions company, recognised for transforming ideas into impactful brands, profitable opportunities, and memorable customer experiences.
